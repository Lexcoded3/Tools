HOW TO USE
----------
In PixelForge: paste a version into the "Prompt" field — either alone or
APPENDED after your subject ("a vintage watch, " + one of the blocks below).
The one-liner below is the strongest general version; the categorized
blocks help you fine-tune for a specific subject (nature vs objects vs
people close-ups). Keep the whole prompt under ~2000 characters — the free
Perchance backend caps the prompt length.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 PRO TIPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Pair people close-ups with the "Negative prompt" field — paste from
   negative-prompt.txt so the model never drifts into deformed faces,
   extra fingers or anime skin. They are designed to work together.

2. Guidance scale: 7–12. Lower (6–9) keeps texture natural; higher
   (10–12) sharpens fine detail but can over-polish skin.

3. For a believable macro look, include ONE camera phrase (e.g. "macro
   lens, shallow depth of field") — it signals framing + lens behavior,
   not just "zoom".

4. "Fill the frame" is the single strongest zoom cue. Add it before any
   detail words if the subject keeps coming out small.

5. Keep prompts under ~2000 characters (Perchance backend limit).
   The short Version 2 is ideal for 16/32-image batches.

6. Fixed seed + fixed prompt = comparable results — change one variable
   at a time to see which detail tokens actually help.

7. Style pairing: "Photo" or "No style" works best for realism.
   Skip styles like "Anime" or "Pixel Art" unless a stylized close-up
   is the goal.
