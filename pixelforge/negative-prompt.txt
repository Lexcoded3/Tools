 CATEGORIZED — UNDERSTAND WHAT EACH BLOCK FIXES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ HANDS & FINGERS ]   ← the #1 failure in Stable Diffusion
    extra fingers, missing fingers, fused fingers, webbed fingers,
    extra hands, malformed hands, twisted hands, clawed hands,
    deformed hands, hand disfigurement, too many fingers, too few fingers

[ LIMBS & BODY ]      ← prevents extra or missing arms/legs and weird joints
    extra limbs, missing limbs, extra arms, extra legs, missing arms,
    missing legs, malformed limbs, misplaced limbs, fused limbs,
    duplicated limbs, severed limbs, amputee, amputated, dismembered,
    contorted body, twisted body, warped body, broken anatomy

[ FACE & HEAD ]       ← keeps faces human and symmetrical
    mangled face, distorted face, disfigured face, ugly face,
    deformed face, asymmetric face, asymmetric eyes, cross-eyed,
    deformed iris, pupil deformation, missing eyes, extra eyes,
    duplicate face, cloned face, multiple heads, two heads, no head,

[ QUALITY & CLEANLINESS ]  ← standard quality tokens (always include)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 PRO TIPS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Weighted anatomy (if your model supports A1111/Comfy syntax):
   (deformed hands:1.4), (extra fingers:1.3), (bad anatomy:1.2)
   PixelForge's free Perchance backend ignores weights, so use plain
   comma lists there.

2. If hands still fail, add to the POSITIVE prompt:
   "perfect hands, five fingers, detailed hands, anatomically correct"

3. For portraits only, add: "no body, bust shot, head and shoulders"
   For full bodies, drop the face block down to just
   "deformed, disfigured, bad anatomy, extra limbs, extra fingers"

4. Guidance scale: keep it 6–9. Too low (<5) lets deformities slip
   through; too high (>12) can cause over-sharpened, plastic skin.

5. Same seed + same negative = reproducible results. Use the 🎲/S key
   to test a few seeds until a batch comes out clean.

6. A fixed (non-random) seed + a fixed style is the best way to compare
   whether a new negative token actually helps — change one variable at
   a time.
